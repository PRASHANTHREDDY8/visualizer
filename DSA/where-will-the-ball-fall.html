1706. Where Will the Ball Fall

Medium Array Matrix Simulation O(m × n) O(n) space

Understanding the Problem

Problem

A box has m × n cells, each containing a diagonal board:

  • \ (value 1) — redirects the ball to the right
  • / (value −1) — redirects the ball to the left

Drop a ball at the top of each column. For each ball, find which column it exits from at the bottom, or −1 if it gets stuck.

When Does a Ball Get Stuck?

  • V-shape trap: Two adjacent cells have opposing diagonals (\/), forming a V. The ball is caught at the bottom.
  • Wall collision: A \ at the rightmost column or a / at the leftmost column pushes the ball into a wall.
Key Insight: A ball passes through a row only if the current cell and the adjacent cell (in the redirect direction) share the same diagonal direction. We simulate column-by-column, row-by-row.
