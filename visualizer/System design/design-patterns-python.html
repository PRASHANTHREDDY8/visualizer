★ Overview

Design patterns are reusable solutions to common software design problems. They were formalized by the "Gang of Four" (GoF) in 1994 and have since been adapted to modern languages. Python's dynamic typing, first-class functions, and metaclasses make many patterns more concise — and some even unnecessary.
