Key Insight

A 90° clockwise rotation equals transpose (swap rows ↔ columns) followed by reversing each row. Both steps are in-place swaps — no extra matrix needed. Alternatively, you can rotate layer-by-layer using a 4-way cyclic swap.

Approach 1: Transpose + Reverse

Step 1: Transpose — swap matrix[i][j] with matrix[j][i]
Step 2: Reverse each row

Approach 2: Layer Rotation

Process concentric rings from outside-in. In each ring, perform a 4-way swap cycling elements through their rotated positions.
